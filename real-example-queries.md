# Realistic LLM Prompts

Variant 1 — Single Buyer, ~$525K Budget
I’m looking in the $500K–$525K range, and within that budget I need a detached or semi‑detached home built after 1990 with at least 3 bedrooms and 2 full bathrooms. A single attached garage is required—street parking isn’t practical in winter. The lot doesn’t need to be large, but something around 3,000–4,000 sq ft would keep things comfortable. I want to avoid properties located within 150 meters of high‑voltage transmission lines, and I’m not considering homes in designated flood fringe or floodway zones along the Bow or Elbow River. Being within 1 km of grocery stores or restaurants would be ideal, but I don’t want to be directly adjacent to commercial parking lots or loading bays.
If possible, I’d like the home to be within 800 meters of a bus stop or C‑Train station, and I’d prefer a neighbourhood with posted speed limits of 40 km/h or lower to keep noise down. A backyard with at least 15 meters of depth would be nice, and if the home happens to have roughed‑in A/C, that’s a bonus even if it’s outside the typical price range.\

Variant 2 — Couple, ~$675K Budget
We’re shopping in the $650K–$675K range and want a detached home built after 2000 with 3+ bedrooms and 2+ bathrooms. A double attached garage is important, and we’d like a minimum lot size of 4,500 sq ft. We’re avoiding homes located within 200 meters of major transmission corridors, and we don’t want anything backing directly onto Deerfoot Trail, Stoney Trail, or other high‑noise corridors. Being within 1.5 km of restaurants, cafés, or a small commercial plaza is a plus, but not directly adjacent to them.
We’d prefer a home that’s at least 300 meters away from riverbanks or stormwater ponds, just to avoid moisture‑related concerns. Access to a C‑Train station within 10 minutes by car would be ideal. If the home includes a finished basement with permits, that’s great, and if it has central A/C, even better—though we know that might push the budget slightly.

Variant 3 — Family of Four, ~$850K Budget
We’re targeting the $800K–$850K range and need a detached home built after 2010 with 4 bedrooms and at least 2.5 bathrooms. A double attached garage is mandatory, and we’d like a lot size of 6,000–7,500 sq ft to give the kids space. We want to avoid homes located within 250 meters of high‑voltage lines or within mapped flood hazard zones near the Bow or Elbow River. We’d also prefer not to be within 100 meters of stormwater ponds, just to avoid mosquito and moisture issues.
Being within 1 km of an elementary school and 2 km of a grocery store would be ideal. We’d also like to be within 500 meters of a neighbourhood park or pathway system. Access to major roads is important, but we don’t want to back directly onto them. If the home includes a bonus room above the garage or a walk‑in pantry, that’s great. A triple garage would be outside our expected price range, but if it appears, we’d consider it.

Variant 4 — Downsizing Couple, ~$600K Budget
We’re aiming for the $575K–$600K range and want a detached home built after 1995 with 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. A single or double attached garage is required. A lot size around 4,000 sq ft is sufficient. We want to avoid homes located within 150 meters of transmission lines and anything in flood fringe areas. We’d prefer to be within 1 km of restaurants or a small retail plaza, but not directly adjacent to commercial dumpsters or delivery zones.
We’d like the home to be within 800 meters of a park or pathway, and ideally within 10 minutes of a C‑Train station. If the home has a deck already built or landscaping with established trees, that’s a plus. A roughed‑in gas line for BBQ or central A/C would be nice, even if slightly outside the typical range for this price bracket.
